# PawPass portfolio direction

## Purpose
A fictional pet retail and care brand that demonstrates the experience a local pet store could offer online. No real sales, fulfillment, provider verification, or customer endorsements are implied.

## Customer journeys
1. Discover essentials → filter by pet/category → product details → persistent bag → sample checkout → demo receipt.
2. Explore care → editable pet profile → sample provider → scheduling → saved demo booking.
3. Learn about the brand → explore pet-parent journal → read FAQs and sample store information.

## Information architecture
Home, Shop (homepage collection with categories/search/sort), Pet care, Our story, Pet-parent journal, Help. Product details, bag, demo checkout, and store information open as accessible dialogs.

## Visual direction
Warm cream, ink/plum, terracotta, sage. Editorial serif headings, restrained sans-serif copy, local original vector product illustrations, pet photography, generous spacing, and tactile rounded surfaces. No fabricated ratings, reviews, certification claims, addresses, or phone numbers.

## Delivery phases
1. Brand and content foundation — this document, reusable product catalog, navigation.
2. Homepage and footer — two clear purposes, pet categories, collection, care, story, journal, FAQs, substantial footer.
3. Product browsing — category/pet filters, search, sort, product details.
4. Shopping demo — persistent bag, quantity/remove actions, shipping estimate, local sample receipt. No personal information or payment details required.
5. Care — retain functional local booking demo, integrate into overall site.
6. Verification — TypeScript/build, desktop/mobile browser checks, shop and care flows, keyboard/dialog behavior, storage failure feedback.

## Customization for a buyer
Replace catalog, illustrations/photos, currency, business identity, actual services, policies, and contact links. Then connect inventory, payments, tax/shipping, order storage, and communication integrations appropriate to that business. The sample checkout intentionally makes no external request.
